= What's new

QALIPSIS is designed with the scalability and simplicity required to perform load, efficiency and end-to-end verification on both distributed and stand-alone systems

With QALIPSIS:

* You can create scenarios with different branches and running with many minions.
* You can use its many native steps and plugins to fire load on your system and feed QALIPSIS with data to verify load strength.

QALIPSIS can run in embedded, standalone and distributed modes. It has the capability to log and save a history of events and metrics using various tools such as Elasticsearch, Graphite, TimescaleDB and InfluxDB.

In future QALIPSIS releases, you can expect an expansion of plugins, an increase in load injection steps and verification processes and various other improvements.

Feedback on this release, including bugs and suggested enhancements, is welcome and may be sent to the QALIPSIS team at https://github.com/qalipsis[Github, window=\"_blank\"].

== Version history

All the public versions of QALIPSIS OSS are available on the https://mvnrepository.com/artifact/io.qalipsis/qalipsis-platform/versions[Maven central repository].

=== v0.18.f — 2026-10-08

==== Highlights

- **JUnit report reworked** — a campaign is now reported like a test suite: one test case per step, skipped when the
  step was not executed, failed or errored according to the severity of its messages, with the step status, executions,
  messages and measurements attached as standard output. The whole campaign is written to a single file.
- **Campaign configuration validated before submission** — the configuration rules, such as the maximum number of
  zones, are now shown and enforced directly in the form instead of being discovered through a rejected submission.
  Zone configuration is explicitly optional.
- **Clearer zone configuration** — zones are sorted by label and fully described, with their flag and description, and
  default to a 100% share.
- **Campaigns no longer hang** — a campaign with no remaining factory to acknowledge its shutdown terminates
  immediately, and a scenario that was never closed takes the final status of its campaign instead of being reported as
  running forever.
- **New extension point** — an additional campaign hook is triggered when the warm-up of the scenarios starts, the
  point from which a campaign effectively consumes resources.
- **TimescaleDB plugin** - The values of the timer meters were read as microseconds instead of the correct milliseconds.

==== Fixes

- Generating the report of a campaign configured with a zone failed with a template error; zone resolution was also
  made significantly cheaper.
- Requesting the abort of a campaign whose factory could not be created or could not start left the campaign stuck in a
  non-terminal state. The abort is now honoured whatever the state of the factories, and the delay before the
  cancellation is propagated is configurable.
- The completion hook was not properly called when a campaign failed at start.
- Clearer labels on the deletion buttons of the reports and series pages.
- Refinements to the scenario report display, and web application dependencies upgraded.
- Internal meters are now prefixed with an underscore.

=== v0.18.e — 2026-07-06

==== Highlights

- **HTML execution reports** — a campaign can now produce a self-contained, user-friendly HTML report, generated
  automatically at the end of a run or downloaded from the campaign details page. It details the campaign summary and,
  for each scenario, its own summary, the zones it ran on with the configured load distribution, and every visible step
  in execution order with its measurements and messages.
- **Textual report for standalone runs** — the same results are also available as a readable console/text report.
- **Reports without an external time-series database** — campaign measurements are now collected and stored by the
  Head, so standalone runs produce complete reports out of the box.
- **Accurate meters** — meters are no longer aggregated when read for charts and reports: step measurements keep their
  original granularity, and the campaign-level values are returned alongside for comparison. As a consequence, a
  data-series built on meters no longer takes an aggregation operation.
- **Scenario and step details in the web UI** — the campaign details page now shows the same breakdown as the report,
  once the campaign has completed.
- **Slack and Email plugins** - Graphical improvements to notifications and email notifications can now transport the textual, the HTML and the JUnit reports when enabled.

==== Fixes

- The timer step compared values using the wrong time unit.
- The minion identifier was attached as a tag to the timeout failure meter, inflating its cardinality.
- The campaign, data-series and report details screens could not be refreshed.
- Steps that were not executed were not displayed as disabled.
- Various report generation failures.

==== New configuration
